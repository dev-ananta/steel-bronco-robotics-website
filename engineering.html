Engineering

Ideas become hardware.

The engineering process is the product. Students learn to make decisions, test assumptions and improve a system until it works.

Build-test-revise cycles expose failure modes before competition and teach engineering judgment.

Scouting and match analysis connect engineering choices to the game that the robot actually has to play.
