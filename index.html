02 · Engineering

From CAD to competition.

Students define the problem, design it, manufacture it, wire it, program it, and then drive it under match pressure.

DESIGN

Mechanical + CAD

Iterate mechanisms around real constraints: geometry, weight, reliability and cycle time.

MAKE

Manufacturing

Welding, 3D printing, CNC machining, fabrication and assembly turn ideas into hardware.

CONTROL

Electrical + software

Students wire, program, test and troubleshoot systems that must work when the clock is running.
